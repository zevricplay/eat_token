❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is a Free Fire EAT Token?

EAT (External Auth Token) is an authentication token used by Free Fire/Garena to verify your account. It's used to perform account actions like changing your bio.

What is a Free Fire JWT Token?

JWT (JSON Web Token) is a more secure token format derived from your EAT or Access Token. It's required by the Free Fire to authenticate requests.

Is this Free Fire Token Generator safe?

Yes, 100% safe. We use the official Garena OAuth login — your password is never shared with us. We only convert the token you already have.

How to get my Free Fire EAT token?

Log in using the provider buttons above, then check your browser history for URLs containing 'eat='. and usually found in the redirected URL after login.
